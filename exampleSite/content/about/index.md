Spudlab aims to make scientific data easier to gather, preserve, inspect, and use.

## How we work

We are committed to open development, scientific integrity, and reproducible methods. Data sources, units, timing, transformations, and limitations should be traceable. Experimental work should be labelled honestly, and mistakes should be corrected visibly.

## Contribute

Useful contributions include code, documentation, testing, accessibility improvements, scientific review, calibration work, hardware experiments, and specific feedback.
